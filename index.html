About Tech Me Academy

Tech Me Academy is an online academy that teaches practical IT skills through recorded classes, and helps learners turn those skills into jobs across the GCC.

We started in Dubai in 2026 with a simple observation: busy IT professionals and students often miss live training. Recorded classes fix that. You watch when you can, pause when you need to, replay what you missed and practise with labs.

Our classes cover cloud computing, networking, cybersecurity, DevOps and automation, system administration, programming, databases, data and AI, IT support, and IT management and soft skills. Every learning path moves from the basics to hands-on work, so you can use what you learn at your job the next day.
